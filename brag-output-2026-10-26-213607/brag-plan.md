# Brag Plan: PreScripto

## What is this app?
PreScripto is a clinic management system for patient registration, consultations, and prescriptions.

## The angle
A composed, human view of the clinic day moving from a patient queue through a clinician consultation to a print friendly prescription.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The existing PreScripto mark resolves into “FROM QUEUE TO CARE.” over the app’s deep slate and cyan palette.

## Key moments
- A clearly fictional sample patient record appears with queue token and clinical vitals.
- The consultation workspace moves from notes to a typed or drawn digital prescription.
- The queue and print friendly prescription output close the loop.

## Outro / punchline
“Patients. Consultations. Prescriptions.” followed by PreScripto branding.

## User flow worth showing
Queue entry → consultation and vitals → digital prescription and print friendly output. All patient and clinician names are fictional stand-ins.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, confident clinic workflow launch film
- Interpretation: measured movement, restrained accents, clean product UI.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: #090d16 (composition palette)
- Accent: #22d3ee cyan, #3b82f6 blue
- Text: #ffffff / #cbd5e1
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Strongest visual element: consultation desk and prescription canvas

## Share copy
PreScripto brings patient management, consultations, and print friendly prescriptions into one clinic workflow.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained upbeat bed with a few quiet UI accents
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves vol. 10
- Music treatment: low bed at 0.38, smooth fade at end
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue file at composition/assets/music/cues.json; major reveal aligns near 3.01s, consultation near 8.22s, and brand close near 18.55s where readability allows.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none in inherited composition
- SFX posture: sparse, low risk
- Audio-coupled moments: patient card reveal and prescription confirmation
- Restraint rule: no alarm sounds or busy repeated ticks.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Queue to care — 3.2s
Brand hook “FROM QUEUE TO CARE.”
Sequential/interaction: title resolves in two readable beats.
Audio intent: warm, confident opening.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet switch accent on reveal.
Transition mood: clean → consultation.

### Scene 2 — Consultation — 5.0s
Show a fictional sample record, vitals, consultation workspace, and prescription canvas. Text labels describe actual workflow without medical instructions.
Sequential/interaction: record and prescription panels arrive in sequence.
Audio intent: light UI texture.
Audio-coupled idea: soft clicks on the two panel reveals.
Transition mood: soft → queue.

### Scene 3 — Queue and print — 4.6s
Show token queue and print queue with A4/PDF output labels.
Sequential/interaction: token then print panel.
Audio intent: calm confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: one restrained confirmation sound.
Transition mood: clean → patient management.

### Scene 4 — Patient records — 3.2s
Show patient and clinic management framing with visit records and prescription history.
Sequential/interaction: query and result card reveal.
Audio intent: quiet and informative.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle response accent.
Transition mood: soft → close.

### Scene 5 — Brand close — 4.0s
PreScripto by Zevox identity and grounded feature line.
Sequential/interaction: logo, name, then tagline.
Audio intent: resolve and fade.
Audio-coupled idea: soft logo landing.
Transition mood: clean.

**Music mood:** polished upbeat
**Audio summary:** A restrained instrumental bed supports three product reveals and fades under the logo.
