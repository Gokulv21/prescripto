# Brag Plan: PreScripto by Zevox

## What is this app?
PreScripto is an ultra-fast, multi-tenant clinical operations & intelligent digital prescription suite designed for modern clinics and hospitals — featuring zero-lag patient queues, digital stylus prescriptions, waiting-room TV chimes, and the Nova AI clinical co-pilot.

## The angle
High-speed modern healthtech: Show how PreScripto replaces slow, fragmented clinic management with a sleek, unified, Apple-grade interface that takes a patient from entry to printed prescription in under 60 seconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-contrast glowing dark mode badge with rapid counter ticks: "CLINIC MANAGEMENT IS BROKEN." pulsing into "MEET PRESCRIPTO."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: Instant Consultation & Stylus Rx:** Token #04 calls in, digital prescription pad accepts typed medicines and stylus drawing with 0ms latency.
- **Beat 2: Waiting Room TV Chime & Thermal Print Queue:** Audio-coupled token chime for the waiting room TV display and immediate thermal/A4 print generation.
- **Beat 3: Nova AI Co-Pilot:** Real-time clinical analytics and bilingual assistant (Tamil & English) answering doctor operations queries live.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero lag. Zero paper chaos. Welcome to the future of healthcare." — PreScripto by Zevox.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Patient triage token generation with vitals (BP 120/80, SpO2 99%).
2. **Action:** Doctor consultation desk with rapid protocol matching and digital prescription.
3. **Delivery:** Automatic print queue dispatch and waiting room TV notification.

- Preset: `polished`

- Interpretation: Elegant typography, subtle glassmorphic glows, precise rhythmic cuts on beat hits, and zero fluff.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook & The Reveal — 3.2s
- **Visual:** Deep slate background with ambient cyan radial gradient. Bold typography slams in: "CLINIC MANAGEMENT IS BROKEN." Glitches/swaps into "MEET PRESCRIPTO." Floating VisionOS dock badge emerges.
- **Sequential:** Words hit on 0.82s and 1.90s beats.
- **Audio:** Music kicks off; crisp whoosh/switch sound at 1.90s.
- **Transition mood:** Hard snap → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Consultation Desk & Stylus Rx — 4.8s (t=3.2s to 8.0s)
- **Visual:** High-fidelity UI mockup card showing Token #04 with vitals (BP 120/80, SpO2 99%), interactive medicine pills (`Amoxicillin 500mg`, `Paracetamol 650mg`), and an active digital prescription canvas drawing stroke.
- **Sequential:** Patient card slides in at 3.2s; medicines pop sequentially on 4.10s, 4.64s, 5.19s; signature/stylus path animates at 6.28s.
- **Audio:** Soft card drops and keyboard clicks.
- **Transition:** Sweep wipe → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — TV Queue Display & Thermal Print — 4.5s (t=8.0s to 12.5s)
- **Visual:** Split-screen showcase: Left side shows the Waiting Room TV Display with big golden token `#04` and live audio chime wave. Right side shows the centralized Thermal Print Queue with "PRINT SENT: 0.2s".
- **Sequential:** TV token pulses at 8.73s; Print status stamp locks in at 10.38s.
- **Audio:** Audio chime accent at 8.73s.
- **Transition:** Clean slide → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Nova AI Clinical Co-Pilot — 3.0s (t=12.5s to 15.5s)
- **Visual:** Nova AI intelligent search card with emerald glow badge: "Ask Nova anything...". Prompt typed in: *"Nethu ethana patients vandhanga?"* Instant answer card pops up with live analytics stats and multi-tenant security verification.
- **Sequential:** Query enters at 12.56s; answer card springs in at 13.64s.
- **Audio:** Digital interface beep / select click.
- **Transition:** Dramatic fade-in zoom → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Outro & Logo Hero — 2.5s (t=15.5s to 18.0s)
- **Visual:** PreScripto emblem logo and bold typography: "PreScripto by Zevox". Tagline: "The Operating System for Modern Healthcare." CTA: "Experience Zero-Lag Healthcare."
- **Sequential:** Logo lands on 15.82s strong beat; subtitle reveals on 16.38s.
- **Audio:** Music resolves with smooth fade-out.
